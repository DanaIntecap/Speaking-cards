English Journey Speaking Cards

Skill: Speaking

Activity Type: Pair and Group Speaking Practice

Estimated Time: 5–8 Minutes

About this Activity

This activity is designed to help students develop confidence and fluency in spoken English through short interactive conversations. Students participate in pair work or small group discussions using randomized speaking cards related to the selected unit. The activity promotes active communication, vocabulary retrieval, and meaningful interaction in English.

Learning Objective

Develop confidence and fluency in spoken English through short interactive conversations using vocabulary, expressions, and language structures from the selected unit while participating actively in pair and group interactions.

Instructions

  1. Select the unit you want to practice.
  2. Click 🎲 New Card to display a random question.
  3. One student reads the question aloud or uses the audio button.
  4. Another student answers the question using a complete sentence whenever possible.
  5. After answering, ask at least one follow-up question to continue the conversation.
  6. Switch roles and continue interacting with your partner or group members.
  7. Use 🌎 Translation only when necessary to support comprehension.
  8. Compare your ideas with the ✅ Suggested Answer.
  9. Review the 💡 Reminder for important language points.
  10. Continue generating cards until the assigned practice time is completed.

Follow-Up Questions Reminder

Keep the conversation going! A conversation is more than one answer.

  • What about you?
  • How about you?
  • And you?
  • Why?
  • When?
  • Where?
  • Who with?
  • Really? Tell me more.
  • Can you explain more?
  • What happened next?

Success Criteria

  • I answer using complete sentences whenever possible.
  • I use vocabulary and expressions from the selected unit.
  • I participate actively in the conversation.
  • I ask at least one follow-up question.
  • I communicate in English throughout the activity.
  • I help maintain the conversation with my partner or group.
🏆 Speaking Challenge

For each card:
  1. Answer the question.
  2. Ask a follow-up question.
  3. Keep the conversation going for at least 30 seconds before moving to the next card.
